# NEXORA help

NEXORA is one shared app for SACCOs (savings and credit co-operatives). One login covers every SACCO you belong to.

## Account and email
- Register with your email and a password. NEXORA emails you a confirmation link. It works once and expires in 24 hours. If it is missing, check spam, then ask for a new link on the verify page. A new link can be requested once every 60 seconds.
- Forgot your password: use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page. The reset link expires in 1 hour and works once. Resetting or changing your password signs you out everywhere.
- Changing your email address means the new address has to be confirmed again.
- Five wrong passwords in a row lock sign-in for 15 minutes.

## Joining a SACCO
- A SACCO officer adds you by email. You receive a join code by email. The code works once and expires after a few minutes, so use it soon.
- Sign in, open "Join with a code" and enter the code. You may be asked to read and accept the SACCO's rules, and to set a four-digit PIN.
- If the code expired or was used, ask the officer to send a new one. A new code replaces the old one.

## Entering a SACCO
- After signing in, choose a SACCO and enter its four-digit PIN. Too many wrong PINs lock the PIN for 15 minutes; an admin can reset it.
- What you can see and do depends on your role in that SACCO. Pages you cannot use are not shown.

## Savings, deposits and withdrawals
- "My savings" shows your balance and history in the SACCO.
- "Deposit" and "Withdraw" send a request. Money only moves in the app, after the request is handled. This assistant cannot move money, approve anything, or change anything for you.
- Some SACCOs take deposits by M-Pesa. The SACCO's pay-in details show where to pay.
- Wrong entries are corrected by a reversal that an officer requests and a second officer approves, not by editing history.

## Loans
- Loans are requested in "Loans". A loan needs approval by an officer other than the one who prepared it. The SACCO's rules set limits.

## Privacy and help
- Do not type passwords, PINs or ID numbers into this chat.
- For anything about a specific payment or dispute, contact your SACCO officer.
